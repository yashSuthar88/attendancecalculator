How to Use

Welcome to the official user guide for ATTENDCALC. Learn how to get the most out of our tools.

📊 Attendance Calculator

The Attendance Calculator gives you an immediate snapshot of where you stand and whether you have room to miss your next class.

  • Attended: Enter the exact number of classes you have been present for.
  • Total Held: Enter how many classes have happened so far.
  • Target Attendance: Set the minimum percentage you need to maintain (e.g., 75%).
Pro Tip: The calculator instantly tells you exactly how many upcoming consecutive classes you can safely skip, or exactly how many you must attend to catch up.

📅 Attendance Predictor

The Attendance Predictor is a predictive tool. It uses your regular weekly timetable to project the exact date in the future when your attendance goal will be reached.

  • Timetable: Fill in the number of lectures you have on each specific day of the week.
  • Weekly Off Day: Automatically zeroes out a specific day if your college is always closed.
  • Saturday Type: Handle tricky schedules by telling the system if your college is off on all Saturdays, odd Saturdays, or even Saturdays.
  • Public Holidays: Add any upcoming unpredicted holidays so the system doesn't count them as lecture days!
How it works: The engine simulates moving forward day-by-day based on your daily slots, continuously checking your percentage until it hits your target goal.

✈️ Holiday Planner

Planning a trip but worried about how much it will hurt your attendance? The Holiday Planner calculates the precise drop.

  • Trip Dates: Select your departure and return dates.
  • Daily Slots: Same as the Attendance Predictor, enter your default daily class count.
  • Schedule Settings: The planner intelligently ignores your college off-days and weekend rules so it doesn't over-punish you for a trip!
The Verdict: The system will give you a color-coded "Impact Verdict" letting you know if the trip's damage to your record is Low, Moderate, or High.

📉 Absence Impact Calculator

The Absence Impact Calculator helps you understand exactly how much your attendance will drop if you miss a specific number of upcoming classes.

  • Current Status: Enter your current 'Attended' and 'Total Held' classes.
  • Classes to Miss: Input the number of upcoming lectures you are planning to skip.
  • What-If Scenario: Instantly see your new expected percentage and the net drop.
Why use it? This tool is perfect for when you need to skip just a few classes for an appointment, event, or personal reason and want to know the damage.
