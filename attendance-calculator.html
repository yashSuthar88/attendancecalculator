General Attendance Criteria

Most universities mandate a strict minimum of 75% attendance to be eligible for end-semester examinations. Falling below this threshold typically results in an automatic academic detainment.

A relaxation of up to 10% (bringing the minimum requirement to 65%) is generally only considered for severe medical emergencies or official representation in college-sanctioned events (like sports, cultural activities, or NSS/NCC). These exceptions always require valid documented certificates and prior administrative approvals.
