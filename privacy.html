🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. All calculations (attendance percentage, predictions, holiday impact) are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No data is sent to, processed on, or stored on any server. We do not use accounts, logins, or registration of any kind.

2. Cookies & Web Beacons

attendancecalculator.me uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and to record user-specific information on which pages the site visitor accesses or visits. This helps us optimize the user experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type or other information.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on attendancecalculator.me.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings. (Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info).

4. Data Security

Since we don't collect or store any data, there is no risk of your information being breached, leaked, or misused through our platform. Your numbers stay on your device and are discarded when you close the tab.

5. Children's Privacy

Our tools are safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
